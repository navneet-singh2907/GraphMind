HelioDesk customer FAQ

HelioDesk is a fictional product used as a GraphMind demonstration corpus.

Does HelioDesk send answers directly to customers?

No. It drafts an answer with source references. A support agent must review and confirm any external reply.

What happens if the relationship graph is offline?

Search continues with semantic and exact keyword retrieval. The interface labels graph evidence as unavailable, so the degraded mode is visible rather than silently ignored.

Which plan supports SAML SSO and European data residency?

Enterprise includes SAML SSO and lets a tenant pin content and audit data to the eu-west region. Starter and Growth store demo tenant data in us-east.

How long are audit records retained?

Starter retains records for 30 days, Growth for 180 days, and Enterprise for 400 days.

What is the support escalation path?

Priority support acknowledges a severity-one incident within 30 minutes. The support lead opens an incident channel, the owning on-call team investigates, and the incident commander publishes an update every 60 minutes until mitigation.

Can a customer request source deletion?

Yes. Deleting a source removes it from the next canonical ingestion output. The associated vector records and extracted graph facts are then rebuilt from the remaining source set.
